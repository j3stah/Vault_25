Finally I was plunged deep into a yielding pile of soft light brown dirt and sighed. I hung there for a moment, allowing the dirt on all sides to hold me up. I let my forehead fall forward into the pile and listened to the sounds of cave. All I could hear was the sound of static, a muffled breath all around of something pressed in by layer after layer of the past, like a stack of history books all fallen into each other due to their mass. I looked up for the hole I had fallen through but it had gone. The pile of dirt has also disappeared, sifted away bit by bit into cracks in the floor I couldn't see. I shivered, I felt I had been standing in the top half of an hourglass and it is an ancient evil thing to trap someone in a clock. I was left in there in the clearing, covered in a layer of dirt. There were lanterns lighting the clearing, but the light did not extend into the tunnels that branched away into the world. I walked over to one and traced the swirls of the glass with my finger, feeling how warm the glass was. Nobody to light it, I wondered to myself. I considered snuffing it out to see what would happen, peer through the veil to see how it was lit in the first place but I withdrew my hand from the flame. I was worried I would fall through the floor again.
