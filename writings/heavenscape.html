Heavenscape

(The time I remembered not getting to go on a ride and started feeling salty about it lmao)

At some point when I was young, I was at some carnival or fair. It might have been a family outing, more likely a friends birthday. We were all getting into some sort of bumper-car-esque ride. Something where we were all getting into our own pods excited to zoom around under the flashing lights. At the last moment, a worker came over and told me that mine was broken. I couldn't ride in it. I had to sit on the bench until the ride was over and then I could trade with someone else. I waited, watching my friends dash off into the colored lights on the horizon, the electronic music pulsing through the air around them. I started to feel as if I was surrounded by something insidious. Seeing my friends in such a sugary trance from the music, eyes trailing with color. By a lucky chance I was not there, also immersed, parading the same steps and chanting the laughs. Letting it draw me out farther into a land of sweet fading music. It just disturbed me. And I saw my friends throw their heads back in laughter as the music blared...

If there exists the concept of a hellscape, then this garish arcade must be the poisoned syrup that is a heavenscape. An equal and opposite horror. A subtle evil. A creeping realization that life was never meant to be this good without dying or meeting the devil first.
