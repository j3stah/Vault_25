One Star Boot

Dream I had when I very young

I am walking home along the side of the highway on a small dirt path. It is sunny and warm. But it is very wrongly quiet. I feel compelled to suddenly reach down a hole in the dirt nearby that appears to be a ground squirrel hole. I watch in third person as I, in a backpack and school uniform, reach down into the hole and pull out a small boot with a small star-shaped spur near the heel. It is a "one-star" boot. The world around me shivers. There's a twisting sense that the boot is a very bad omen and that something is wrong. The grass around me is silent. The road next to me is empty. The sun continue to shine down, more orange than before. I feel a deep sense of dread but I continue walking home. Now I am at home. I am upstairs standing on a balcony attached to my room. I am watching myself in third person from outside and I can see my room behind me as my body looks out from the balcony. I see an empty closet on the other side of the room. It's doors are open. A single wire hanger is hanging from a metal bar. All of a sudden a faceless white mannequin appears in the closet. The flannel shirt it is wearing is wrapped around the hanger. It's neck is at an awkward angle. It's head wears a cowboy hat. It is wearing one star boots. My body still leans on the balcony edge looking outwards. It starts silently moving towards me, hands outstretched to choke me. At the last second I spin around and manage to kick it into the stairwell. I start violently cursing out of fear and adrenaline, but now it can't harm me anymore.
