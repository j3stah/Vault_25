Electric Land

I'm leaning back in a plastic chair in a strange reality. All around me are sloped columns made black gridlines. They form a gride of sloped holes in the floor and ceiling in all directions. Purple and white crackles around each columns and pulses through the floor and ceiling A constant waterfall of numbers and electric charge pours straight down from the sky, pressing me into my chair from the weight The charges course my body and fill me with sparks that fling themselves my fingers. The sky and floor start to close together while the chair and my head start to stretch apart. I start viewing my own thoughts three steps ahead of myself. The loops that my mind goes through are so obvious to me now. I can view my thoughts as blue lines shining out away from me like a rhythmn game in reverse. They branch into each other and stack. The thoughts about my thoughts are higher up and the thoughts about those thoughts are even higher. I am able to judge myself clearly. Every evaluation fresh and not clouded with past shortcuts and assumptions. I am in third person. I see my building complex as a translucent yellow maze and see myself inside with an exaggerated head. I see how unremarkable I am compared to those around me. I fall through different abysses of realization about my potential and my flaws. In some worlds I am a genius because of my ability to "imagine" and "process the world differently". In others I realize that I have not created anything and I am not a math genius adept at visualizing problems. In some worlds I am fine with being myself and enjoy the experience of being me. I accept not being the most powerful. Having an enjoyable expereience is the priority. In others I face the hard truth that I am not good at anything. I try to run from this truth because of how painful it is and I cannot escape. I think about how much I procrastinate. How much of a side character I am. How underveloped and shallow I am compared to the people I looked up to in high school. Existential crisis. I feel guilty about how fake and inauthentic I am to everyone. I recognize that I have good taste but that doesn't translate to anything much.
